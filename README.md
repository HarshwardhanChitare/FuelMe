# FuelMe
Fuel stations across India remain largely dependent on operation, where attendants handle both payment collection and fuel dispensing. This dependence contributes to customer waiting times during peak hours, higher operational costs for station owners and an increased likelihood of human error in transactions. This paper presents the problem formulation for a QR code based self service fueling system intended to address these issues by improving customer convenience, reducing transaction time and enabling digital management for fuel station owners. The proposed system uses QR codes to identify and authenticate individual fuel pumps, simulates a digital payment flow and demonstrates the logic for automatic fuel dispensing once payment is confirmed, along with digital receipt generation and transaction record keeping. Five research questions guide the study covering authentication security, the hardware and software components required for a working prototype, reductions in transaction time safeguards against fraud and the value of real time transaction monitoring for station owners. A review of work on RFID based and IoT based self service fuel systems shows that most existing research favors RFID tags, biometric verification or dedicated hardware tokens leaving a gap for a lighter weight smartphone based QR approach that suits the Indian retail fuel context, where QR based digital payment is already widely adopted. This paper outlines the scope, the expected outcomes of a simulated prototype and a software architecture designed to be extendable to payment gateways and certified dispensing hardware in future work.
